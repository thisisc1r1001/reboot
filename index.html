From drawer to desk in four steps

Every device is checked, cleaned, and handed over by student volunteers.

  1. Donate

    Tell us what you have. Drop it at a campus collection point.

  2. Wipe

    We erase all personal data and confirm it with you.

  3. Refurbish

    Volunteers repair, update, and test each device.

  4. Hand over

    A student who requested a device receives it.

Questions

Is my data safe?

Every device is wiped before refurbishing. We share the wipe confirmation with you.

Who can request a device?

Any student with a genuine need. Requests are reviewed by volunteers and matched in order.

What if my device is broken?

Tell us in the notes. Small repairs are fine, and parts from unusable devices help fix others.

Does it cost anything?

No. Devices are donated and handed over free.
